HOW I CAN HELP

Practical support for the moving parts of your business.

Whether you're setting up a CRM for the first time or improving a process that has become difficult to manage, the goal is the same: build a system your team can actually use.

01 / CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT

GoHighLevel CRM Setup & Management

Build a clear home for your leads and customers. I configure your GoHighLevel account, pipelines, opportunity stages, tags, custom fields, calendars and user access so your team can see what needs attention and what happens next.

  • Account setup and configuration
  • Sales pipelines and contact organisation
  • Custom fields, tags and opportunity stages
02 / FOLLOW-UP & OPERATIONS

Workflow Automation

Reduce the manual work involved in responding to enquiries and keeping customers informed. I build workflows that trigger the right follow-up, reminder or internal notification when something happens in your business.

  • Email and SMS follow-up sequences
  • Missed-call text-back and appointment reminders
  • Lead routing, notifications and nurture workflows
03 / LEAD GENERATION

Funnels & Landing Pages

Create a clear path from first visit to enquiry or booking. I build landing pages, forms and simple funnel journeys that capture the information you need and connect each new lead to the right follow-up process.

  • Landing pages and funnel steps
  • Lead capture forms and surveys
  • Connected CRM and follow-up journeys
04 / SCHEDULING

Appointment & Booking Systems

Make it easier for customers to book and for your team to stay prepared. I configure calendars and booking pages, then connect confirmations, reminders and post-booking communication to reduce manual coordination.

  • Booking calendars and appointment pages
  • Confirmation and reminder workflows
  • Team notifications and follow-up
05 / CONNECTED TOOLS

Integrations & CRM Migration

Help the tools you already use work together. I connect platforms and move customer information carefully, reducing disconnected processes and making important data easier to manage.

  • Make.com, Zapier and n8n workflows
  • Webhooks, APIs and platform connections
  • Contact imports and CRM migration
06 / CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE

Email Marketing, AI & Reporting

Support better communication and clearer visibility across your customer journey. I help set up email campaigns, review engagement and pipeline activity, and connect AI chat or voice tools where they serve a practical purpose.

  • Email campaigns and nurture sequences
  • Workflow and pipeline reporting
  • AI chat and Vapi voice workflow integrations
