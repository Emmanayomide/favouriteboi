MY STORY & WORKING MINDSET

More than setting up software. I focus on how the business actually works.

Every business has a series of important interactions: someone discovers the business, makes an enquiry, receives a response, books a call, becomes a customer and hopefully returns. When those steps are scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes, calendars and disconnected applications, it becomes harder to keep track of people and deliver a consistent experience.

That is the kind of operational challenge I enjoy working on. I look at the journey from the first enquiry to the next meaningful action, then help put the right structure in place. That may mean organising contacts and sales stages in GoHighLevel, building an automated follow-up sequence, connecting a booking calendar, or making two business applications share information reliably.

I approach projects with curiosity, attention to detail and a practical mindset. I want to understand what the business needs before recommending a tool or building a workflow. A system should solve a real problem, be understandable to the people using it and be maintainable after implementation.

01 / CLARITY

Make the process visible

Organise information and stages so people can understand what is happening and what needs attention.

02 / USEFULNESS

Automate with purpose

Use automation to remove repetitive steps and support timely, relevant communication.

03 / RELIABILITY

Test the full journey

Check triggers, conditions, messages and handoffs instead of assuming a workflow works.
