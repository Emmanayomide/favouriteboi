THE PERSON BEHIND THE SYSTEMS

Hi, I'm Emmanuel Ayomide.

I'm Emmanuel Ayomide, a CRM & Business Automation Specialist focused on helping businesses create better ways to capture leads, manage relationships and follow up with confidence.

A PRACTICAL, PROCESS-LED APPROACH

I care about what happens after a lead comes in.

A new enquiry is only the beginning of a customer journey. Someone needs to receive it, understand what the prospect is looking for, respond at the right time, keep the conversation organized and make sure the next step is clear. When those details are spread across inboxes, spreadsheets and disconnected apps, opportunities can slip through the cracks.

That is the kind of operational problem I enjoy working on. I help bring the moving parts into a more connected process: a CRM that reflects the business, follow-up that happens consistently, booking journeys that are easy to use and automations that support the people doing the work.

GoHighLevel is central to much of my work because it brings together CRM, funnels, calendars and communication workflows. I also use integration platforms and other tools when a business needs its existing systems to work together rather than start over.

What I bring to a project

I approach CRM and automation work as a business process, not just a software task. Before choosing triggers or building pages, I think about the people using the system and the journey a lead or customer will experience. A useful setup should make the next action clearer, reduce repetitive administration and give the team a dependable place to work from.

Depending on the project, that can mean building a CRM foundation from the ground up, improving an existing GoHighLevel account, connecting lead sources, creating a funnel or designing the follow-up that happens after someone submits a form or books a call.

01 / CRM FOUNDATION

Organized customer data

Configure pipelines, stages, tags, custom fields, contact records and opportunity management so teams can understand where each lead stands and what should happen next.

02 / AUTOMATION

Consistent follow-up

Build email and SMS workflows, lead assignment, nurture sequences, appointment reminders, missed-call text-back and internal notifications to reduce avoidable manual work.

03 / CONVERSION

Clear customer journeys

Create landing pages, forms, surveys, booking pages and funnel steps that guide prospects from initial interest to a meaningful next action.

04 / CONNECTED TOOLS

Systems that communicate

Connect platforms using Zapier, Make, n8n, webhooks and APIs, with tools such as Stripe, calendars, email services and lead sources where appropriate.

05 / AI-ASSISTED WORKFLOWS

Useful AI experiences

Support lead engagement and operations with practical chatbot and voice-agent workflows, including qualification, routing and handoff to a human when needed.

06 / VISIBILITY

Reporting and refinement

Help teams monitor pipeline activity, campaign engagement and workflow performance, then improve the setup based on what the data and day-to-day use reveal.

How I work: understand first, automate second

I do not believe every manual task needs an automation, or that adding more software automatically makes a business more efficient. I start by understanding the current process, identifying where time or information is being lost, and deciding what should happen at each stage. Then I build and test around that plan.

01

Understand the business process

Map the journey from lead source to follow-up, appointment, sale or handoff. Identify bottlenecks, repeated tasks and information gaps.

02

Plan the right structure

Define the CRM fields, pipeline stages, triggers, communication steps and integrations before connecting everything together.

03

Build and connect

Configure the CRM, pages, workflows and supporting tools in a way that is understandable and maintainable.

04

Test real scenarios

Check what happens when a lead submits a form, replies, books, cancels or needs a human response. Refine any gaps discovered during testing.

The person behind the work

I'm based in Abuja, Nigeria, and work remotely with businesses, founders and agencies beyond my location. I hold a B.Tech in Chemical Engineering from Ladoke Akintola University of Technology (LAUTECH), which I completed in 2023. Although my professional work is now focused on CRM systems and business automation, my engineering education continues to shape how I approach problems: break a complicated process into understandable parts, look for the cause of the friction, and build a solution that works as a whole.

My hands-on work has included GoHighLevel account setup and configuration, sales funnels and landing pages, email and SMS automation, calendar and appointment workflows, contact organization and CRM migration support, and campaign review. I have also worked on connecting the customer journey across lead capture, follow-up and booking. I describe my role and contribution accurately, and I do not present a project as a complete end-to-end result when the scope was only one part of the wider system.

What matters to me in client work is being dependable, communicating clearly and leaving people with a system they can understand. I like solving practical problems, learning the details of how a business operates and making the technology feel less complicated for the people who rely on it every day.

Outside the technical build, I value thoughtful communication and continuous learning. I believe good automation work requires both technical curiosity and the patience to understand the humans on the other side of the workflow.
