MY PROFESSIONAL FOCUS

Technology should make running a business simpler.

I work at the intersection of customer relationship management, business processes and automation. My focus is not simply adding more software to a business. It is understanding how the business attracts leads, responds to enquiries, manages conversations, books appointments and follows opportunities through to the next stage — then configuring systems that support those activities.

GoHighLevel is a key part of my toolkit, particularly for businesses and agencies that need their CRM, funnels, calendars and follow-up processes working together. I also work with connected tools and automation platforms where they make the overall setup more useful.

What I bring to a project

A good CRM should reflect the way a business actually works. I pay attention to the structure behind it: what information is captured, how contacts are organized, who owns each opportunity, what should happen after an enquiry and how the team can see what needs attention.

01 / CRM FOUNDATION

Organized customer data

Configure pipelines, stages, tags, custom fields, contact records and opportunity management so teams can understand where each lead stands and what should happen next.

02 / AUTOMATION

Consistent follow-up

Build email and SMS workflows, lead assignment, nurture sequences, appointment reminders, missed-call text-back and internal notifications to reduce avoidable manual work.

03 / CONVERSION

Clear customer journeys

Create landing pages, forms, surveys, booking pages and funnel steps that guide prospects from initial interest to a meaningful next action.

04 / CONNECTED TOOLS

Systems that communicate

Connect platforms using Zapier, Make, n8n, webhooks and APIs, with tools such as Stripe, calendars, email services and lead sources where appropriate.

05 / AI-ASSISTED WORKFLOWS

Useful AI experiences

Support lead engagement and operations with practical chatbot and voice-agent workflows, including qualification, routing and handoff to a human when needed.

06 / VISIBILITY

Reporting and refinement

Help teams monitor pipeline activity, campaign engagement and workflow performance, then improve the setup based on what the data and day-to-day use reveal.

How I think about implementation

I believe a reliable system starts with clarity. Before building, I want to understand the current process, the people who will use it and the outcome the business needs. That helps avoid automations that look impressive but create confusion for the team.

01

Understand the business process

Map the journey from lead source to follow-up, appointment, sale or handoff. Identify bottlenecks, repeated tasks and information gaps.

02

Plan the right structure

Define the CRM fields, pipeline stages, triggers, communication steps and integrations before connecting everything together.

03

Build and connect

Configure the CRM, pages, workflows and supporting tools in a way that is understandable and maintainable.

04

Test real scenarios

Check what happens when a lead submits a form, replies, books, cancels or needs a human response. Refine any gaps discovered during testing.
