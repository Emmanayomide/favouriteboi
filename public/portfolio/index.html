FEATURED PROJECT / 01

Lead response & follow-up workflow

WORKFLOW AUTOMATION
Workflow builder · GoHighLevel

A structured follow-up sequence designed to help a business handle new enquiries more consistently after a form is submitted.

PLATFORMGoHighLevel
FOCUSLead management & follow-up

What was configured

  • A form-submission trigger to start the workflow.
  • Contact tagging to organise the new lead.
  • An internal notification so the team is alerted.
  • Wait steps and email actions to support timed follow-up.

Implementation focus

The workflow brings several follow-up actions into one connected sequence. Its structure makes the next steps easier to track and reduces the need to manually initiate each action.
