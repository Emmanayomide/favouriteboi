A note on these samples

CRM SETUP / PIPELINE MANAGEMENT

Lead management CRM

A structured GoHighLevel workspace with organized contacts, custom fields, tags, opportunity stages, ownership and clear pipeline visibility. Designed to help a team track new enquiries, follow-ups and active opportunities without losing context.

What this can include

  • Pipeline and opportunity stage configuration
  • Contact segmentation, tags and custom fields
  • Lead assignment and internal notifications
  • Dashboard views and basic reporting

WORKFLOW AUTOMATION / FOLLOW-UP

Lead response and nurture system

An automated follow-up journey that responds when a new lead comes in, keeps the CRM updated and helps the team follow up consistently. Timing, conditions and handoff rules can be tailored to the business.

What this can include

  • Instant email or SMS acknowledgement
  • Timed follow-up and nurture sequences
  • Appointment reminders and no-show follow-up
  • Reply detection, stop rules and team alerts

FUNNELS / LANDING PAGES

Lead capture and booking funnel

A focused conversion journey that gives visitors a clear next step, captures the right information and connects submissions to the CRM and calendar. Useful for consultations, service enquiries, lead magnets and appointment-based businesses.

What this can include

  • Landing page, forms and survey setup
  • Calendar and booking page configuration
  • Confirmation pages and reminder workflows
  • Lead source tracking and CRM handoff

INTEGRATIONS / CONNECTED SYSTEMS

Connected business operations

A connected workflow that moves information between the CRM and the tools a business already relies on. The aim is to reduce duplicate entry, keep records aligned and make handoffs more dependable.

What this can include

  • Zapier, Make and n8n automation scenarios
  • Webhook and API-based connections
  • Payment, calendar and email integrations
  • Error checks, alerts and workflow documentation
